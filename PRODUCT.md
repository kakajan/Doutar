# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Lovers of traditional Turkmen music, poetry enthusiasts of Magtymguly Pyragy, contemporary world music listeners, and the global Turkmen diaspora seeking high-fidelity digital access to cultural heritage through modern web technology on desktop and mobile devices.

## Product Purpose

"Miras Sahrasy" (میراث صحرا / Heritage of the Desert) is a dedicated music portfolio and streaming web application for an album by Aytronic. It immortalizes the 18th-century philosophical and romantic poetry of Magtymguly Pyragy through a fusion of traditional Turkmen instruments (Doutar, Gyjak) and contemporary cinematic electronic arrangements, providing free 320kbps listening and downloads.

## Positioning

Unlike generic music streaming platforms or static MP3 blogs, Miras Sahrasy delivers an immersive, cultural auditory and visual tribute: a reactive Web Audio visualizer tuned to traditional frequencies, responsive bilingual typography (Persian & Turkmen/English), and zero-friction listening without logins or ads.

## Operating Context

Visitors access the site either to explore and listen immediately (quick listening on mobile/desktop), read the poetic verses in sync with the mood, or download the full studio album (Unity & CHAP! collections) for offline listening.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Audio Engine**: HTML5 Audio + Web Audio API (`AudioMotion Analyzer`) for real-time frequency analysis.
- **Offline Delivery**: Pre-packaged high-bitrate ZIP archives for full album downloads.
- **Localization**: Right-to-Left (RTL) primary interface in Persian with Turkmen/Latin poetic titles and names.
- **Performance**: High frame rate animations (Motion), GPU-accelerated canvas particles, and lightweight initial payload.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Identity**: Solemn, poetic, respectful of Turkmen cultural heritage, yet forward-looking and modern.
- **Palette Identity**: Turkmen Desert Sand, Mulberry Wood Charcoal, Polished Instrument Gold, and Antique Parchment.
- **Typography**: Authentic Persian/Arabic typography (`Vazirmatn`) paired with classical display lettering (`Cinzel`).

## Evidence on Hand

- 58 complete audio tracks curated in three thematic collections: *Unity* (اتحاد - 27 tracks), *CHAP!* (چاپ! - 17 tracks), and *Joda Mandam* (جدا ماندم - 14 tracks).
- Full lyrics excerpts from Magtymguly Pyragy including the poignant separation verses of «جدا ماندم».
- Authentic visual assets including high-resolution album covers and portrait artwork of Magtymguly Pyragy.

## Product Principles

1. **Music First**: Audio playback is continuous, sticky across routes, and never disrupted by navigation.
2. **Cultural Reverence without AI Slop**: The aesthetic honors traditional craftsmanship without relying on generic AI clichés (purple neon glows, fake status chips, or decorative gradient text).
3. **Frictionless Generosity**: All tracks are freely streamable and downloadable in highest fidelity with a single click.
4. **Accessible Craft**: High contrast text (WCAG AA compliant), clear touch targets, keyboard navigable controls, and respectful motion preferences (`prefers-reduced-motion`).
